# Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of ecommerce business performance by analyzing sales, profit, product quantity, customer contributions, regional performance, payment preferences, and product sub-category profitability.

The primary objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can help business owners make data-driven decisions, improve profitability, and optimize business operations.

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Total Amount by State

Analyzes sales performance across different states to identify the highest revenue-generating regions.

### 2. Total Quantity by Category

Displays the distribution of product quantity across Clothing, Electronics, and Furniture categories.

### 3. Profit by Month

Tracks monthly profit performance to identify profitable and loss-making periods throughout the year.

### 4. Total Amount by Customer Name

Identifies top customers based on their contribution to total sales.

### 5. Total Quantity by Payment Mode

Analyzes customer payment preferences across COD, UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and EMI.

### 6. Profit by Sub-Category

Highlights the most profitable product sub-categories to support inventory and product strategy decisions.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Business Performance

- Total sales amount is **438K**.
- Total profit generated is **37K**.
- Total quantity sold is **6K**.
- The dashboard provides a quick overview of the business's overall sales and profitability.

### 2. State-wise Sales Performance

- **Maharashtra** is the highest sales-generating state.
- Madhya Pradesh ranks second in sales contribution.
- Uttar Pradesh and Delhi also contribute to overall sales.
- Regional sales analysis can help identify opportunities for expansion and targeted marketing.

### 3. Category-wise Product Demand

- Clothing contributes approximately **63%** of total quantity.
- Electronics contributes approximately **21%**.
- Furniture contributes approximately **17%**.
- Clothing is the leading category in terms of product demand.

### 4. Monthly Profit Analysis

- January and November show strong positive profit performance.
- May, July, September, and December show negative profit.
- Profitability fluctuates throughout the year, indicating a need to investigate costs, discounts, and product performance during loss-making months.

### 5. Customer Sales Contribution

- Harivansh is the highest sales-contributing customer among the displayed customers.
- Madhav, Madan Mohan, Shiva, and Vishakha are also important contributors.
- Customer-level analysis can help businesses improve customer retention and develop targeted marketing strategies.

### 6. Payment Mode Analysis

- COD accounts for approximately **44%** of total quantity.
- UPI contributes approximately **21%**.
- Debit Card contributes approximately **13%**.
- Credit Card contributes approximately **12%**.
- EMI contributes approximately **10%**.
- COD is the most preferred payment mode among customers.

### 7. Sub-Category Profitability

- **Printers** generate the highest profit among the displayed sub-categories.
- Bookcases rank second in profit contribution.
- Saree, Accessories, and Tables also contribute to profitability.
- The business can focus on profitable product sub-categories to improve overall profit.

## Business Benefits

This dashboard provides several benefits to business owners:

- **Sales Performance Monitoring:** Track total sales, profit, and quantity sold in one place.
- **Profitability Improvement:** Identify loss-making months and investigate the reasons behind declining profit.
- **Inventory Optimization:** Prioritize high-demand categories such as Clothing.
- **Regional Business Strategy:** Identify high-performing states and potential growth opportunities.
- **Customer Retention:** Understand top customers and develop loyalty strategies.
- **Payment Strategy:** Understand customer payment preferences and improve checkout experience.
- **Product Strategy:** Focus on profitable sub-categories such as Printers and Bookcases.
- **Data-Driven Decision Making:** Make informed decisions using interactive visualizations and KPIs.

## Conclusion

The Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard provides valuable insights into sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, regional contribution, and product demand.

The analysis reveals that Maharashtra is the leading state in sales, Clothing is the most demanded category, COD is the most preferred payment mode, and Printers are the highest profit-generating sub-category in the displayed comparison.

However, monthly profit analysis highlights certain loss-making periods that require further investigation.

Overall, this Power BI dashboard enables business owners to monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, optimize inventory, improve profitability, and make smarter business decisions through data-driven insights.
